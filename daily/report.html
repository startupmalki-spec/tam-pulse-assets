Markets performance & news
Global
U.S. stocks rallied on Monday, boosted by solid manufacturing data and optimism around earnings and U.S.-Iran peace hopes. Nasdaq increased 2.1%, while S&P 500 climbed 1.5%, and Dow Jones closed up 1.3%. Asian stocks showed mixed trend on Tuesday morning, with CSI 300 up 0.9%, while Nikkei 225 was down 0.6%. Hang Seng fell 0.5%.Reuters
Saudi
TASI rose 1.1% (117.8 points) to close at 10,823.62. Trading value increased by 68.6% compared to the previous session. The Consumer Services sector was the top performer, rising 4.1%. Among the top advancers, National Company for Learning and Education (NCLE AB) and Rabigh Refining and Petrochemical Co. (PETROR AB) gained 10.0% each, while Saudi Industrial Export Co. (SIECO AB) closed higher by 8.6%.Tadawul
Economy
Saudi Arabia's July S&P Global Composite PMI came in at 53.1, slightly below June's 53.3 reading. Non-oil private sector growth remained in expansion territory for a fourth consecutive month, supported by rising order volumes despite a decline in export demand, the PMI survey showed.Zawya
Economy
Around 95.3 percent of online purchases in Saudi Arabia were made through local retailers, compared with 55.4 percent through international shopping websites. Clothing and footwear were the most purchased online products at 87.7 percent, followed by cosmetics at 46.2 percent, groceries at 41.8 percent, computers and smartphones at 36.9 percent, and travel services at 36.5 percent.Zawya

Corporate news
Petrol Nass Factory (NAASPETR AB) signed a lease agreement with the Saudi Authority for Industrial Cities and Technology Zones (MODON) to lease an industrial land plot in Al Kharj Industrial City, for a period of 20 years, for the purpose of establishing the company's petrochemical manufacturing plant. The annual lease value amounts to SAR 51,598.4. The financial impact of this agreement is expected to commence upon the completion of the construction works and the commencement of the plant's commercial operations.Tadawul
Arabian Internet and Communications Services Co. (SOLUTION AB) signed a contract with Digital Data Centers for Data and Telecommunications Company (Center3) (Related Party) to establish, build, and prepare datacenters (DDC307) in Dammam. The contract carries a value of SAR 364.0 mn, with a duration of 18 months. The financial impact will appear from 1Q27.Tadawul
Al Moammar Information Systems Co. (MIS AB) won a project by the Capital Market Authority (CMA) worth SAR 80.9 mn, including VAT. The project aims to deliver comprehensive managed services for the operation and enhancement of IT services and is expected to be signed on Sept. 3.Argaam
