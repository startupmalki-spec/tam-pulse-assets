Markets performance & news

Global

U.S. stocks showed a mixed trend on Wednesday on signs of progress toward a peace deal with Iran, meanwhile SpaceX and AMD stumbled following their quarterly earnings. The Dow Jones rose 0.5%. S&P 500 fell 0.2%, while Nasdaq declined 0.8%. Asian shares were on a declining trend on Thursday morning after an AI-driven surge the previous day, while oil prices traded in a tight range as markets assessed prospects for an Iran peace deal. The Hang Seng declined 1.2%, Nikkei 225 dropped 1.7%, while CSI 300 fell 0.4%.(Reuters)

Saudi

TASI rose 0.3% (29.4 points) to close at 10,887.55. Trading value decreased by 15.1% compared to the previous session. The Capital Goods sector was the top performer, gaining 1.3%. Among the top gainers, Sport Clubs Co. (SPORTCLU AB), Wataniya Insurance Co. (WATAN AB) and Allied Cooperative Insurance Group. (ACIG AB) each rose 10.0%, followed by United Cooperative Assurance Co. (UCA AB) which increased by 7.8%.(Tadawul)

Sector

King Fahad Industrial Port in Yanbu has received a major shipment of wind energy equipment as part of Saudi Arabia's ongoing efforts to expand renewable energy capacity. The shipment included 27 turbine blades and additional equipment required for wind-based electricity generation.(Zawya)

Economy

Saudi investments expanded 5.1% in 1Q26. Private and public-sector investments continued to pour into new fixed assets and infrastructure in Saudi Arabia amid ongoing economic expansion efforts. Bulk of spending comes from the non-government sector, according to MISA.(Zawya)

Corporate news

Non-institutional foreign investors cut their ownership in Tadawul-listed equities, excluding Saudi Aramco (ARAMCO AB), from 11.3% to 11.2%, or SAR 341.6 bn, in the week ended July 30.(Argaam)

Al Moammar Information Systems Co. (MIS AB) secured a SAR 50.7 mn project, including VAT, from Al-Noor Specialist Hospital in Makkah, part of Makkah Health Cluster. The project covers the operation, maintenance, and repair of medical devices and equipment at Al-Noor Specialist Hospital in Makkah, and the contract is expected to be signed on Sept. 6.(Argaam)

Saudi Arabian Refineries Co. (SARCO AB) said the non-binding memorandum of understanding (MoU) with UK-based renewable energy company AGR to purchase 100% of the output of the green ammonia plant has expired without the parties entering into a binding agreement. The expiration of the MoU does not result in any financial obligations.(Argaam)

Theeb Rent a Car Co. (THEEB AB) BoD approved establishing a limited liability subsidiary in Bahrain, as part of the company's strategy to expand across regional markets and strengthen its operations outside Saudi Arabia. The company said it will own 99% of the new entity, while CEO and board member Naif Al-Theeb will hold the remaining 1%, in line with Bahrain's regulatory requirements.(Argaam)
