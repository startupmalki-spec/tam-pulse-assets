Markets performance & news

Global

Asian shares rose on Monday morning while investors kept a wary eye on oil prices, as the lack of progress towards ending the Iran conflict kept inflation risks tilted to the upside. The Hang Seng index climbed 1.6%, while CSI 300 and Nikkei 225 index increased 0.8% and 0.4%, respectively.(Reuters)

Saudi

TASI rose 0.9% (96.1 points) to close at 10,919.68. Trading value decreased by 23.4% compared to the previous session. The Financial services sector was the top performer, rising 2.3%. Among the top advancers, Naseej International Trading Co. (NASEEJ AB) and Raydan Food Co. (RAYDAN AB) rose 10.0% each, while Tihama Advertising, Public Relations and Marketing Co. (TAPRCO AB) increased 7.3%.(Tadawul)

Economy

Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (HRSD), in partnership with the Ministry of Municipalities and Housing (MOMAH), announced a decision to raise the Saudization rate for project management professions in the local private sector to 70%, effective Feb. 14, 2027. The decision applies to all private-sector establishments employing three or more workers in the targeted professions.(Argaam)

Corporate news

Qassim Cement Co. (QACCO AB) signed a SAR 400 mn financing agreement with the Saudi Industrial Development Fund (SIDF). The deal aims to finance the construction of a fourth production line with a production capacity of 10,000 tpd to replace some of the existing production lines at the company's plant. The financing has a 9-year tenor, including a 2-year grace period and a 7-year repayment schedule.(Argaam)

Al-Jouf Agricultural Development Co. (JADCO AB) signed agreements to supply French fries to companies operating AlBaik restaurants. The agreements cover the supply of French fries, with quantities and prices to be determined quarterly. The agreements represent an important strategic step for the company given the expected business volume. The company expects the agreements to have a positive financial impact, depending on the development of agreed supply volumes and actual orders.(Argaam)

Scientific & Medical Equipment House Co. (EQUIPMEN AB) announced the award of the catering services project for armed forces hospitals for 3 years in the Western Region, valued at SAR 127.7 mn. The financial impact of this contract is expected to be reflected in 2Q27.(Tadawul)

Wajd Life Trading Co. (WAJDLIFE AB) signed a contract worth SAR 39.3 mn with Imam Mohammad Ibn Saud Islamic University (IMSIU). The three-year contract covers the operation and maintenance of medical equipment and clinics at the university's campus. The project is expected to have a positive financial impact on Wajd Life over the next three years.(Argaam)

Modern Mills Co. (MODERNMI AB) signed a Shariah-compliant credit facility agreement worth SAR 23.5 mn with Saudi Awwal Bank (SABB AB). The facility will be used to finance its new feed mill project at Al-Jumum and support its working capital. The financing is divided into a Capex financing project worth SAR 120.0 mn, with a tenure of eight years, and a revolving credit facility worth SAR 115.0 mn, with a one-year tenure.(Argaam)
