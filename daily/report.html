Markets performance & news

Global

U.S. stocks closed lower on Monday as investors waited for quarterly reports from large retailers to provide insights into U.S. consumer spending, while oil prices rose as the U.S. and Iran appeared no closer to a deal. The S&P 500 and Dow Jones declined 0.5%, each, while Nasdaq declined 0.3%. Asian stocks were also down on Tuesday morning, while bond yields climbed to decade highs. The Nikkei fell 1.9%, followed by CSI 300 and Hang Seng index which dropped 0.8% and 0.6%, respectively.(Reuters)

Saudi

TASI fell 0.1% (11.6 points) to close at 10,908.06. Trading value increased by 27.1% compared to the previous session. The Insurance sector was the top laggard, down 1.6%. Among the top laggards, Al Kathiri Holding Co. (ALKATHIR AB) dropped 10.0% followed by Almasane Alkobra Mining Co. (AMAK AB) and Advanced Building Industries Co. (ZIIC AB) declining 4.6% and 4.1%, respectively.(Tadawul)

Economy

Saudi Arabia's crude oil production reached around 3.5 bn barrels in 2025, up 5.6% from a year earlier. Crude output rebounded last year after falling in 2024 to its lowest level in 14 years, according to the General Authority for Statistics report titled Oil and Gas Statistics. Crude oil exports rose 6% in 2025 to 2.4 bn barrels, from 2.2 bn barrels in 2024.(Argaam)

Economy

The seasonally-adjusted Al Rajhi Capital Saudi Construction Index, compiled by S&P Global, advanced to 55.2 points in July 2026, from 56.3 points in June. Saudi construction companies recorded another strong expansion in business activity in July, extending the current period of growth to three months. The index remained well above the crucial 50.0, with the latest reading the second-highest since the survey began in January.(Argaam)

Corporate news

Foreign institutions on the Main Market (TASI) were net buyers of stocks worth SAR 275.1 mn during the week ended Aug. 13. Saudi retail investors were net sellers of shares worth SAR 130.4 mn, while Saudi institutional investors were net buyers of shares worth SAR 47.0 mn.(Argaam)

Saudi Aramco (ARAMCO AB) is offering crude oil outside the Strait of Hormuz to some Asian refiners through private negotiations, two sources with knowledge of the matter said. Aramco is in talks with buyers to supply Arab Medium and Arab Heavy crude via ship-to-ship (STS) transfers off Fujairah in the UAE. Aramco has also offered the grade from Egypt's Mediterranean port of Sidi Kerir following attacks in the Red Sea by the Iran-aligned Houthis.(Zawya)

Saudi Arabian Mining Company (MAADEN AB) announced the signing of an MoU between Maaden Integrated Fertilizer Company, a wholly owned subsidiary of Maaden, and SABIC Agri-Nutrients Co. (SAFCO AB) to develop and invest in opportunities across fertilizers and the integrated agri-nutrients value chain, including the production and manufacturing of value-added products. The memorandum duration is 3 years from the signing date.(Tadawul)

Al-Jouf Agricultural Development Co. (JADCO AB) unveiled details of the agreements to supply par-cooked French fries to companies operating AlBaik restaurants, according to a statement on Tadawul. These contracts were signed with Awaed Al Mashreq Co. Ltd, Ebdaa Watan Food Trading Co., Fast Food Co., Serve Quest Restaurant Management Co., and Al Woustah Food Services Co. The contracts are valid for one year and are renewable. The relevant financial impact depends on the development of agreed supply volumes and actual orders, starting 3Q26.(Argaam)

Middle East Pharmaceutical Industries Co. (AVALONPH AB) announced the execution of a strategic commercialization agreement with Bio-Thera Solutions. Avalon has been granted exclusive rights to register and commercialize Dupilumab. The value of the contract represents less than 5% of the company's revenues for 2025. The contract duration is 15 years. The financial impact is expected to start from the launch date in 2031, estimating cumulative revenue of no less than SAR 400 mn during the first seven years of the contract period.(Tadawul)

Retal Urban Development Co. (RETAL AB) announced that Building and Construction Co. (A subsidiary owned 80% by Retal Urban Development Company) has signed a contract with Remal Park Fund (an associate company) to execute the design and building services works for the Remal Business Court project in Al Khobar city, with a value of SAR 268.0 mn. The contract duration is 12 months. The project is expected to have a positive impact in 2026 & 2027.(Tadawul)

Jamjoom Pharmaceuticals Factory Co. (JAMJOOMP AB) completed its acquisition of a pharmaceutical manufacturing facility located in King Abdullah Economic City, Saudi Arabia, from Pfizer Saudi Limited. The facility specializes in manufacturing and packaging oral solid dosage (OSD) medicines.(Argaam)

Diriyah Co. and Arab National Bank (ARNB AB) signed a SAR 2.0 bn (USD 533.0 mn) financing agreement to develop a portfolio of serviced residences in Diriyah and Wadi Safar, marking the first partnership between the two parties. The agreement will support the development of luxury residential units for several global hotel brands across the Diriyah and Wadi Safar projects, as part of Diriyah's efforts to develop various assets within the 14-square-kilometer Diriyah masterplan and the 62-square-kilometer Wadi Safar masterplan.(Argaam)
