Markets performance & news
Global
U.S. stocks closed higher on Tuesday, powered by the latest batch of earnings from AI-related companies such as Caterpillar and Palantir that assuaged demand concerns. Nasdaq and S&P 500 gained 2.6% and 1.8%, respectively, while Dow Jones increased by 1.7%. Asian stock markets were up on Wednesday morning as robust earnings and renewed appetite for tech propelled Wall Street to record highs, while hopes for progress on opening the Strait of Hormuz pushed down on oil prices and bond yields. Nikkei 225 climbed 3.2%, while MSCI Asia Pacific index and CSI 300 rose 2.2% and 1.0%, respectively.Reuters
Saudi
TASI rose 0.3% (34.5 points) to close at 10,858.14. Trading value decreased by 3.6% compared to the previous session. The Commercial & Professional Services sector was the top performer, rising 1.9%. Among the top advancers, United Wire Factories Co. (ASLAK AB) increased by 9.9%, followed by the Mediterranean and Gulf Insurance and Reinsurance Co. (MEDGULF AB), which gained 9.0%, while Saudi Printing and Packaging Co. (SPPC AB) closed higher by 6.9%.Tadawul
Economy
The General Authority of Foreign Trade (GAFT) has imposed anti-dumping duties on cast iron pipes and hollow tubes (ductile iron pipes) from India. The anti-dumping duties will range from 16.96% to 29.94% of cost, insurance and freight (CIF) value, not less than SAR 714 and SAR 1260/tonne.Zawya
Economy
The hotel room occupancy rate in Saudi Arabia declined to 60.8% in 1Q26, compared to 63% in the same period of 2025. The occupancy rate for serviced apartments and other hospitality facilities inched up to 51.6%, up from 50.7% a year earlier, according to the General Authority for Statistics (GASTAT).Argaam
Consumer
Around 95.3% of online purchases in Saudi Arabia were made through local retailers, against 55.4% through international shopping websites. Clothing and footwear were the most purchased categories at 87.7%, followed by cosmetics at 46.2%, groceries at 41.8%, computers and smartphones at 36.9% and travel services at 36.5%. (Zawya)

Corporate news
Arabian Centres Co. (CENOMICE AB) announced that its Westfield Jeddah project has been completed, received its occupancy certificate, and officially launched operations on July 30. The project had achieved a pre-leasing rate of nearly 96%. Once Westfield Jeddah reaches a stable operating phase, the project is expected to contribute about SAR 350.0 mn in additional EBITDA annually, strengthening the company's net profit and cash flows.Argaam
Saudi Basic Industries Corp. (SABIC AB) completely divested its specialty engineering plastics business in North America, South America and Europe. The divested business recorded operating losses of approximately SAR 1.9 bn for 2025, and around SAR 648.0 mn for the six months ended June 30, 2026. SABIC added that separating the business improved its pro forma EBITDA margin by approximately 130 to 140 basis points.Argaam
