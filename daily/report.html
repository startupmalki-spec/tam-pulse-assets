Markets performance & news

Global

U.S. stocks advanced on Friday, after data showed the U.S. economy unexpectedly shed jobs last month and dampened expectations the Federal Reserve would raise interest rates at its September meeting. The Nasdaq rose 1.3%, and S&P 500 gained 0.6%, while the Dow Jones increased by 0.3%. Asian shares closed higher on Friday, with CSI 300 increasing 0.9%, and Hang Seng rising 0.5%. While, MSCI Asia Pacific index closed higher by 0.2%.(Reuters)

Saudi

TASI fell 0.7% (-76.0 points) to close at 10,811.57. Trading value increased by 10.6% compared to the previous session. The Utilities sector was the top laggard, declining 2.7%. Among the top decliners, Umm Al-Qura Cement Co. (UACC AB) declined 6.3%, followed by Retal Urban Development Co. (RETAL AB) which fell 6.2%, while Saudi Ground Services Co. (SGS AB) closed lower by 5.7%.(Tadawul)

Sector

Saudi Arabia's operating renewable energy capacity increased 88% to 12,313 MW by the end of 2025, following the addition of five new solar energy projects during the year, according to the Renewable Energy Statistics 2025 report. The total number of operated renewable energy projects reached 15 by year-end. The report attributed the increase in capacity to the operation of five new solar energy projects with a combined capacity of 5,762 MW.(Zawya)
